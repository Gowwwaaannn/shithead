BOT DIFFICULTY
2–4 players receive 3 face-down, 3 face-up and 3 in hand. 5–8 receive 2/2/2.

The player holding the lowest non-special card starts and must play that card. A 2, 8 or 10 can never determine who starts. If two or more players share the lowest eligible rank, they each roll two dice; highest total starts (tied highest totals reroll).

2 resets, 8 is transparent, 10 burns. Four equal ranks in sequence also burn. 7 is not a special card: it can only be played when it fits the normal sequence (or after a 2 reset). Once a 7 is played, the next player must play 7 or lower. Special cards can always be played. Once the deck is empty, matching cards may be played together from your hand and face-up row in the same turn.
